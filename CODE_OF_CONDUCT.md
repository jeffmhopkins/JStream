# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone in JStream's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews, including comments that agents post for the maintainer.

<a id="expected"></a>
## Expected behaviour
<!-- @section
-->

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep to the topic, and give the details someone needs to act on what you write.
- Accept that the maintainer decides the project's direction, and that "no" or "not
  now" is a complete answer.

<a id="unacceptable"></a>
## Unacceptable behaviour
<!-- @section
-->

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexual language and imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, and comments meant to intimidate.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Posting secrets or exploit details publicly, instead of reporting them as
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.
- Spam, including automated or AI-generated content posted without care for whether
  it's correct or relevant.

<a id="enforcement"></a>
## Enforcement
<!-- @section
-->

The maintainer ([@jeffmhopkins](https://github.com/jeffmhopkins)) enforces this code. To
report a problem, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. For anything that
involves a security problem, use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may edit, hide or delete content, lock a
thread, or block someone from the repository. Reports are kept confidential.
